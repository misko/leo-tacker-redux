# V5 canary: radio 192.168.1.15

Date: 2026-08-13  
Transport: standard libiio IP/TCP  
RF fixture: TX2 split by an SMA tee to RX1 and RX2; no LNB

## Safety and scope

The canary did not flash firmware, change QSPI, or alter boot configuration.
Before every receive or DDS operation, TX1 and TX2 attenuation were explicitly
set to `-80 dB` and read back. DDS channels were disabled after each injection.
The final state was TX1/TX2 at `-80 dB` with DDS disabled. SSH authentication
was unavailable, so `/opt/VERSIONS` was not collected. No other discovered
radio was contacted.

## IP continuity measurements

All measurements used paired CI16, 262,144 samples per refill, four kernel
buffers, parsed V3 metadata, and explicit sample-sequence accounting.

| Configured rate | Returned frames | Missing source frames | Gapped transitions |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2.083332 MS/s | 30 | 13 | 13 of 29 |
| 2.5 MS/s | 20 | 13 | 13 of 19 |
| 3.0 MS/s | 20 | 18 | 17 of 19 |
| 5.0 MS/s | 20 | 43 | 18 of 19 |

At the minimum rate, 30 returned refills represented 3.775 seconds of IQ but
required 6.301 seconds of wall time. Effective payload throughput was about
10 MB/s versus 16.67 MB/s required for continuous paired CI16. The host NIC
was negotiated at 2.5 Gb/s, so the bottleneck is downstream of that host link.
No overflow flag was asserted; the buffer and sample sequences were the only
reliable evidence of loss.

## Ethernet correction and continuity retest

On 2026-08-14 the radio-side Ethernet cable/path was changed. The same serial,
firmware, host metadata runtime, paired CI16 layout, block size, and four kernel
buffers were retained. The radio then negotiated 1000 Mb/s full duplex instead
of 100 Mb/s.

| Measurement | Before correction | After correction |
|---|---:|---:|
| Radio Ethernet negotiation | 100 Mb/s | 1000 Mb/s full duplex |
| Direct TCP host to radio | 94.1 Mb/s | 730 Mb/s |
| Direct TCP radio to host | 94.1 Mb/s | 769 Mb/s |
| IIO receive throughput | approximately 11 MiB/s | 57--58 MiB/s |
| Returned V5 metadata frames at minimum rate | 30 | 30 |
| Missing source frames | 13 | 0 |
| Gapped transitions | 13 of 29 | 0 of 29 |

The post-correction metadata run requested 2.083332 MS/s, read back 2.083331
MS/s, and returned buffer sequences 0 through 29 with exact adjacent FPGA
sample-sequence increments. The radio was restored to 30.72 MS/s after the
bounded test.

## Decision

The radio is accepted as a V5 functional canary. Ordinary paired IQ, metadata,
gain/RSSI reporting, timing, loopback, and Redux translation work as designed.

The corrected IP path is accepted for a bounded, fail-closed contiguous canary
at 2.083332 MS/s. Redux must still require metadata proof and reject every
unverified or gapped recording. This result does not qualify a 10 MS/s paired
capture, a long-duration production schedule, or an 8--24-hour soak; those
remain separate measured gates.
